### Common Sources of Data Generation

- **Transactional applications**  
  Structured data such as orders, payments, invoices, and customer records.

- **Web and mobile apps**  
  Semi-structured event data such as clicks, page views, sessions, and sign-ups.

- **Databases**  
  Structured data such as inserts, updates, deletes, and change logs.

- **IoT and sensors**  
  Streaming time-series data such as temperature, location, and machine telemetry.

- **Logs and observability tools**  
  Semi-structured or unstructured data such as application logs, audit logs, and metrics.

- **Third-party APIs**  
  Semi-structured data such as JSON payloads, partner records, and enrichment feeds.

- **Files and documents**  
  Structured, semi-structured, or unstructured data such as CSVs, spreadsheets, PDFs, and flat files.

- **Streaming platforms**  
  Event-driven data such as Kafka messages, user events, and system events.
